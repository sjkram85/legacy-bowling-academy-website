Legacy Bowling Academy was founded by Scott J. Kramer, who has been on the lanes since he was four years old. Built out of a lifelong love for the sport and a deep belief in the USBC motto “A Future for the Sport,” the academy exists to share real, honest bowling knowledge with anyone ready to put in the work.

Legacy Bowling Academy is built for every bowler—not just one age group or skill level. Today every session is led directly by Scott, with plans to expand into a collaborative roster of USBC-certified coaches as the academy grows. Whether you're a youth athlete learning sound fundamentals, an adult league competitor chasing higher consistency, a senior bowler refining effortless leverage, or someone who simply has questions about the game, you have a place here.

PROCESS

How Coaching Together Works

Whether you want a full mechanical breakdown or just have a few questions about your game, getting started is straightforward.

STEP 01

Start With Your Questions

Fill out the booking form below with your age group, program, and the times that work. Scott reviews your goals and schedule personally and confirms a lane time at Forest Hill Lanes.

STEP 02

Focused, Plain-English Coaching

We watch your natural game first—never forcing a one-size-fits-all style. Together, we isolate the 1–2 highest-impact mechanical or mental-game adjustments so you leave clear, not overloaded.

STEP 03

Practice With Purpose

Real improvement takes persistence and focus. You walk away with simple drills, clear mental cues, and any pro shop surface or fit notes coordinated with Doc's Pro Shop so every practice game counts.
